Midterm prototype

MagiCard

Mobile battle companion for a Grade 5 learner. This prototype follows one attack round, from the main menu to the Wizard's Trial result.

Try the main task

  1. Tap Play, then Vs AI.
  2. Tap Slash, then a Main Row slot. Do the same for Water Blast.
  3. Tap Guard, then a Support Row slot.
  4. Tap Strike and send the attack.
  5. Solve the Wizard's Trial.
  6. Tap Next turn and decide whether to use Guard.
